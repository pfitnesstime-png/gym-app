Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 23, 2026

At Fitnesstime Gym, we are highly committed to protecting the privacy of our members, website visitors, and staff. This Privacy Policy documents how we collect, store, secure, and utilize your personal information when you use our premium workout applications and physical gym facilities.

1. Information We Collect

To deliver our specialized training guidance, nutrition targets, and membership access, we collect personal credentials which include:

  • Profile Credentials: Your name, phone number, email address, age, gender, weight, and height.
  • Gym Attendance Logs: Visual QR check-in timestamps recorded at our front desk.
  • Fitness Progress Logs: Target metrics, body fat percentages, BMI measurements, and before/after workout tracking photographs.
  • Financial Statements: Transaction reports, billing history, and GST invoice records logged securely on our management console.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your metrics and health goals exclusively to operate and secure your Premium Member Portal, including:

  • Prescribing personalized workout routines and customized diet programs.
  • Verifying active membership validity and counting expiration timelines.
  • Generating digital tax receipts and GST invoice records.
  • Broadcasting relevant operational timings or discount banner announcements.

3. Data Security and Firewalls

Your records are secured utilizing the industry-standard Firebase Security Rules and advanced database firewalls. We maintain rigorous local encryption and do not rent, trade, or transfer your details to any third-party marketing services.

4. Member Choices and Rights

Every member retains the full authority to update their height/weight stats, remove uploaded progress photos, or request immediate profile termination by directly contacting the gym front desk support desk.
